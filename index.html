NG136 Knowledge Graph Ontology

This page describes the identifiers used in a knowledge graph of the NICE guideline NG136, Hypertension in adults: diagnosis and management. The graph was built for an MSc dissertation. The guideline text is copyright NICE and is not included.

Namespaces

  • https://w3id.org/ng136-kg# (prefix ng:): classes and properties
  • https://w3id.org/ng136-kg/rec/: recommendations
  • https://w3id.org/ng136-kg/concept/: clinical concepts
  • https://w3id.org/ng136-kg/status/: statuses
  • https://w3id.org/ng136-kg/external/: external references
